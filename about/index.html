About

Lefty Design is the name I use for the things I design, build, explore, and occasionally take apart.

It is not a studio — it is one designer's practice, built on a simple belief: design is not decoration, it is a philosophy of decisions. Every interface, object or system is a chain of choices, and making those choices well is the actual craft.

The work moves between digital and physical — product and interface design, interactive prototypes, woodworking, CNC and laser fabrication, electronics, reverse engineering, and whatever happens to sit somewhere between those fields.

Some projects start with a problem that needs solving. Some start with a simpler question: could this be done differently? I tend to follow ideas far enough to make them real — designing the interface, building the prototype, machining the part, writing the code, testing the hardware, or figuring out why something that should work doesn't.

This site is a record of that process.

What I take on

I occasionally take on projects where this experience is useful:
