About Vlad

I'm a product designer, maker, and entrepreneur who enjoys turning ideas into real things — both digital and physical.

My work lives somewhere between UI/UX, technology, automation, woodworking, laser engraving, and the constant process of learning how things work.

I build, experiment, improve, and share what I discover along the way. If something I create, explain, or share has been useful to you, you can support my work here.

Every coffee helps fund new tools, materials, experiments, and the next idea worth building.
